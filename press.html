Give Lewy 2020

An independent global fan petition asking the Ballon d’Or organisers to consider an exceptional, explicitly retrospective “Ballon d’Or 2020” recognition for Robert Lewandowski.

Campaign in one paragraph

The Ballon d’Or was not awarded in 2020. Robert Lewandowski finished Bayern Munich’s 2019/20 season with 55 goals in 47 matches, won the Bundesliga, German Cup and Champions League, and later received both the UEFA Men’s Player of the Year award and The Best FIFA Men’s Player 2020. Give Lewy 2020 does not ask for another player’s trophy to be removed. It asks the organisers to consider recognising the unawarded 2020 edition retrospectively.
