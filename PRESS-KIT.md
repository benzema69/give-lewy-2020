# Give Lewy 2020 — Press & Creator Kit

## One-line pitch
2020 had no Ballon d’Or winner. Give Lewy 2020 is an independent fan campaign asking the organisers to consider an exceptional retrospective recognition for Robert Lewandowski’s 2019/20 season.

## Campaign position
- We are **not** asking for another player’s trophy to be removed.
- We are **not** claiming a 2020 vote result existed; the edition was not awarded.
- We are asking for an exceptional, clearly dated retrospective **“Ballon d’Or 2020”** recognition.
- The campaign should stay factual and positive. Do not attack other Ballon d’Or winners.

## Key facts used by the campaign
- 55 goals in 47 Bayern Munich matches in 2019/20.
- 15 Champions League goals in 2019/20.
- Bundesliga + German Cup + Champions League treble.
- UEFA Men’s Player of the Year 2019/20.
- The Best FIFA Men’s Player 2020.
